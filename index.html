About the Historical Edition

The 2010 Master Rule
The Seventh Edition is preserved exactly as published in 2010. No post-2010 legal developments, corrections, revisions, substitutions, or updates are incorporated into the book.

Page references

The printed page number at the bottom of each page is authoritative. PDF page numbers are only an internal viewing mechanism used by the website.

Use of this archive

This website is an archival presentation of the July 2010 edition. It is not intended to represent current South Dakota law or provide legal advice.
